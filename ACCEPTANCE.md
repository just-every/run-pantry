# Pantry acceptance record

**Purpose and minimum outcome.** A US service business replaces QuickBooks Online, Xero or FreshBooks with Pantry. Its owner, bookkeeper and outside accountant keep the books current from live bank and card feeds and captured receipts and bills. They send invoices that get paid by card or ACH, approve and pay bills through a provider, post payroll and processor activity, reconcile, report on the cash or accrual basis, and close each month with only judgement calls left for the accountant. Throughout, they can leave with a restorable archive and an accountant package. The clauses are in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md).

**Status: 18 of 127 clauses done, 45 partial, 64 missing.** The sixteen ledger-foundation clauses (PAN-01 to PAN-16) are LOCAL PROVEN. Each has one flow test through the public HTTP door of the real Worker on Miniflare, with a real D1 database, every migration applied and genuine native sign-ins (`pnpm run test:pantry`, files under `tests/api/`). The browser journey (`pnpm run test:browser`, `tests/browser/`) repeats the flows in the screens from fresh clones through the `rer` verbs at 1440 and 390 pixels wide, then exports, restores into a second installation and compares the books. LOCAL PROVEN means exercised locally; it is not hosted, provider, accountant or tax acceptance.

Every other row names its acceptance journey and the test that will prove it. A test marked "(target)" does not exist yet, and its clause stays partial or missing until that test passes through a public door. Provider journeys run against the provider's sandbox or test mode (Plaid, Stripe, Melio, BILL, Gusto, Rippling, Intuit, Xero, FreshBooks, Avalara, TaxJar, Square, PayPal, Shopify, Mercury, Ramp, IRS IRIS test system), with fakes only at that paid or network edge. Sandbox journeys never establish production access: each provider's production path (CONNECT-001) is proven separately, from a fresh installation, by an owner with no developer accounts.

## Evidence and decision rule

`rer verify` runs every clause's flow test on each push in the Verify workflow; run the browser journey with `pnpm run test:browser`. A clause is done only while its named test passes on the head being released; a partial or missing clause moves to done when its target test exists and passes through a public door. Status changes are recorded here and in CONTRACT.md's status table in the same change.
