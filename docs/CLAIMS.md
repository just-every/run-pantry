Status key: **code** = the capability exists and runs today; **partial** = a narrower version exists (named), the claimed form does not; **contract** = contract, not yet in code.

## Summary

47 claims: **34 code**, **6 partial**, **7 contract, not yet in code**. Counting the partial claims, whose claimed form is not built, 13 of 47 go beyond today's code. The largest gaps are invoice sending and payment collection, Stripe and Gusto connections, Xero move-in and imported attachments. Live bank and card feeds run on self-hosted installations with their own Plaid keys; on RER Managed they wait for its egress relay. Receipt reading likewise runs with the installation's own AI key on self-hosted installations and says "not available yet" on Managed.

"Try the demo" links `https://demo.run-pantry.com/`, Pantry's hosted demo (`deploy-demo.json`, `DEMO.md`), which answered 200 on 17 Oct 2026.
