## Summary

47 claims: **43 code**, **4 partial**, **0 contract, not yet in code**. Counting the partial claims, whose claimed form is not built, 4 of 47 go beyond today's code. The largest gaps are ACH collection proven live on a US Stripe account, attachments from Xero proven against a live Xero organization (built against Xero's documented API; QuickBooks attachments are not carried), and the books agent asking by text and asking people other than the owner (row 23: email to the owner is built; AGENT-002 and NOTIFY-001 are the build priority). Stripe payouts and Gusto payroll run on self-hosted installations (the business's own Stripe key; the installation's own Gusto app); on RER Managed they say "not available yet". Live bank and card feeds run on self-hosted installations with their own Plaid keys; on RER Managed they wait for its egress relay. Card payment from the invoice link is the same: self-hosted installs connect their own Stripe key, and RER Managed shows it as not available yet. Receipt reading likewise runs with the installation's own AI key on self-hosted installations and says "not available yet" on Managed; so does reading the owner's own-words answers to Pantry's questions (its rule checks and numbered answers run everywhere).

"Try the demo" links `https://demo.run-pantry.com/`, Pantry's hosted demo (`deploy-demo.json`, `DEMO.md`), which answered 200 on 17 Oct 2026.
